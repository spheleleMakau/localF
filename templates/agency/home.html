Meet the founder

SEMU

Model, Actress & Founder of Local Faces Talent Agency

Semu is an accomplished South African model, actress and entrepreneur with an impressive career spanning over 15 years in the modelling and entertainment industry.

Throughout her career, she has worked with leading household brands such as Avon, NIVEA and Dark & Lovely Au Naturale, featuring in major advertising campaigns, television commercials and billboards across Africa.

Her modelling portfolio also includes four cover features for Nubian Magazine, alongside appearances in various other magazines across South Africa.

Her acting credits include Netflix productions Smart Casual and The Executive, as well as Mzansi Magic’s Isikweletu, further showcasing her versatility in the entertainment industry.

After more than 15 years of building a successful career, Semu has finally realised her long-standing dream of establishing her own agency, Local Faces Talent Agency.

With a wealth of industry experience and a passion for developing new talent, she is now dedicated to discovering, grooming and empowering the next generation of models, actors and entertainers.

Through Local Faces, Semu hopes to bridge the gap between aspiring talent and real industry opportunities, creating a platform where everyday people of all backgrounds, ages and body types have the opportunity to be seen, developed and represented.
