A local opportunity with purpose

Bring talent development closer to home.

We’re inviting motivated people to explore bringing Local Faces modelling and talent coaching to their own town.

As a local coach, you could mentor aspiring talent, help them develop confidence and professional skills, and connect your community with the wider creative industry.

The opportunity may also provide a way to earn additional income while contributing to a growing talent platform. Potential, responsibilities and practical arrangements are discussed with Local Faces before you decide whether to proceed.
